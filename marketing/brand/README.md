# SceneFind Final Logo

## Primary assets

- `scenefind-app-icon-final.svg` and `.png`: square app icon with a dark background.
- `scenefind-mark-final.svg` and `.png`: transparent symbol for light surfaces.
- `scenefind-wordmark-final.svg` and `.png`: horizontal lockup for light surfaces.
- `scenefind-wordmark-reversed-final.svg` and `.png`: horizontal lockup for dark surfaces.

## Brand colors

- Ink: `#151822`
- Night: `#07090D`
- White: `#F4F7FB`
- Cyan: `#1FC7EA`
- Violet: `#6D2CE8`
- Magenta: `#C034A8`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the thickness of the magnifying-glass ring. Do not add service logos, movie artwork, shadows, outlines, or additional text inside the app icon.
