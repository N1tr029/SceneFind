Seen a short clip and wondered what it came from? Share it with SceneFind.

SceneFind analyzes user-supplied clips and, when the evidence supports a match, returns the title, season, episode, scene timestamp, and available viewing options.

HOW IT WORKS
Share a clip from another app, import a video from your library, paste a public link, or enter a line of dialogue you remember.

SceneFind can show:
• The title
• The season and episode
• The timestamp where the scene begins
• Available viewing destinations

IT WON'T GUESS
SceneFind shows a title only when the evidence supports it. Season, episode, and timestamp appear only when they can be independently corroborated. If something cannot be confirmed, SceneFind leaves it out instead of inventing an answer.

TIMESTAMPS FROM DIALOGUE
Where captions permit, SceneFind aligns dialogue from the supplied clip with subtitle data to locate the scene.

PRIVACY
No account. No ads. No tracking. Nothing is analyzed unless you explicitly submit it, and analysis data is transient.
