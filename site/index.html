Which scene was that?

Share a clip. Get the title, the episode, the timestamp, and where to watch it.

SceneFind is an iPhone app that identifies the original film or television moment behind a clip you found on social media. Share a video from TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube — or import one from your library, paste a link, or type a line of dialogue you remember — and SceneFind works out where it came from.

What makes it different

  • It won't guess. A title appears only when the evidence supports it. Season, episode, and timestamp appear only when they can be independently confirmed — otherwise the field is left out.
  • Real watch links. Destinations are checked against the actual provider page for the same title and episode, and other countries' storefronts are dropped.
  • Timestamps from dialogue. Where captions allow it, the scene is located by aligning the clip's dialogue against a subtitle index rather than by estimating.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is sent unless you share it.
