Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

SceneFind identifies the movie or TV moment behind a clip you share with it. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking. This policy describes exactly what leaves your device, why, and how long it is kept.

What SceneFind processes

Content you explicitly share

When you share a clip, import a video or image, paste text, or submit a URL, that content is sent over HTTPS to the SceneFind backend, which forwards it to the AI providers listed below so the scene can be identified. SceneFind only ever receives content you deliberately hand to it — through the share sheet, the media picker, or by typing it in. It does not scan your photo library, read your clipboard in the background, or monitor other apps.

If you enable transcription, spoken dialogue in the clip you shared is converted to text so it can be matched against subtitle indexes.

An installation identifier

On first launch the app generates a random UUID and stores it in the device Keychain. It identifies the installation — not you — so that your plan allowance can be counted and so the backend can verify, using Apple's App Attest, that requests come from a genuine copy of SceneFind. It is not derived from any device or advertising identifier and is destroyed when you delete the app and its Keychain entry.

Purchase records

When you subscribe or restore a purchase, the signed transaction Apple issues is sent to the backend for verification and to determine your allowance. SceneFind receives the transaction, not your payment method: card numbers and billing details stay with Apple and are never visible to us.

Diagnostics

The backend records operational data such as request counts, timings, and error categories to keep the service running and to enforce rate limits. Shared URLs, media, transcripts, App Attest assertions, and transaction payloads are excluded from logs.

What SceneFind never collects

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Your location
  • Your browsing or app-usage history
  • Advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers

SceneFind does not track you across apps or websites, does not sell or share personal information, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Who else processes your content

To identify a scene, the backend sends the content you shared to:

  • Google (Gemini) — visual and multimodal identification
  • Groq — dialogue transcription and text reasoning
  • Cloudflare — hosts the backend and processes requests in transit
  • A web search provider and public subtitle/episode indexes — queried with short dialogue phrases and title text to confirm an episode, timestamp, or watch link

These providers act as processors for SceneFind under their own terms and are not permitted to use your content to train their models on our behalf. Apple processes your purchases.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing. Nothing is sent unless you share or import it.
  • Photo and speech access. Both are optional and revocable in iOS Settings; SceneFind only reads items you pick.
  • Deletion. Deleting the app removes the installation identifier and local history. To have the allowance ledger tied to your installation deleted, contact us and include nothing more than your request — we will ask for the installation ID shown in Settings.

Children

SceneFind is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
